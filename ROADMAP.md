# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional and does not promise standards certification or a particular release date.

## v0.1.0

- Stabilize the calculation core and validation.
- Publish transparent engineering assumptions.
- Add CI, tests, and open-source project governance files.
- Keep all unverified electrical data clearly labelled.

## v0.2.0 candidates

- Review cable tables with traceable, legally usable sources.
- Model installation method, ambient temperature, grouping, and conductor temperature.
- Add configurable voltage-drop limits and a documented R/X model.
- Improve result explanations and accessibility.

## Future research

- Protective-device coordination and breaking-capacity workflows.
- More detailed load and demand-factor modelling.
- Localization and exportable calculation reports.

Engineering accuracy, licensing, and review take priority over feature count. No future item should be interpreted as a current compliance claim.
