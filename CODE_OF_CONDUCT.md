# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in ElektroHesap respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, especially in engineering calculations.
- Respect project scope, maintainers, and community time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, doxxing, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
