# Changelog

All notable changes to ElektroHesap will be documented here.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-22

### Added

- Preliminary current, cable, voltage-drop, and protection-current calculations.
- A UI-independent, validated calculation core.
- Unit and smoke tests.
- Engineering assumptions and data-verification documentation.
- Initial open-source contribution, security, and community documents.

### Notes

- Electrical tables are currently marked `UNVERIFIED_DATA`.
- No IEC or TSE compliance claim is made.
